## Proposed episode plan (success first, then the mistake)

**Episode 1, When do we go back?** Animated steps as now. Then Ederson v Napoli: stop before the pass ("What should the defender do?"), play, stop as Ederson receives ("Keeper: where now?"), play to see City keep the ball. Then Ederson v Arsenal: nothing on, so he kicks it long. Then Steele v Leicester as the mistake: the open player was there, the pass went toward the covered one. Recap.

**Episode 2, The back-pass rule.** Animated rule steps with 3-second questions. Then Ipswich v Wolves: stop after the keeper's dive ("What does the referee give?"), play to the arm going up, the ball placed near the small box and the whole team on the goal line, then explain the small-box rule. One or two more examples from the Premier League compilation. Then Montreal v Toronto: "Is this allowed?" No; the referee missed it, it should have been an indirect free kick and a yellow card. Header or chest back: animation only, unless a clip is found.

**Episode 3, How to pass it back.** Animated steps as now. Ortega and Ederson v Napoli for the keeper moving wide. Leno open mic for "the keeper calls it": what a keeper actually shouts, then his back-pass at 2:41. Then Chelsea v Arsenal and Japan v USA as the mistakes.
